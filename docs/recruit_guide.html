Chikai no Sanshi Recruits

Every officer and craftsman who can join the Mouri in Mouri Motonari: Chikai no Sanshi (PS1, Koei 1997), episode by episode, with exactly what triggers each one. Worked out from the game's own event scripts while translating it, so the conditions are what the game checks, not guesses from play.

"X meets Y"The two units come into contact on the battle map. The script checks the pair either way round, so X can walk up to Y or Y can come to X. Only the named unit works.
MissableCan be lost for good. Usually the officer is defeated, the battle ends, or a choice turns them away.
TownTalk to the person in the town or castle scene before the battle, sometimes over several visits. The episode range shows how long the chance stays open.

Notes

Episode numbers are the game's own ("Episode 5: …"). Town-scene recruits are listed under the first episode they can be recruited in; the range shows the last.

Battle-map squares are given as the script's (x, y) grid numbers, counted from 0. If a square seems wrong, try the same numbers the other way round.

Officers who join on their own (Motonari's sons, retainers' heirs and so on) are listed too, marked "story", so the roster is complete.

Not recruitable, although a profile suggests it: Watanabe Kayo (Ep. 7). His "joined" profile carries a developer note saying the feature was only planned, and no script adds him.

Craftsmen: from Ep. 21 (Terumoto's chapters) every town craftsman still left can be hired just by talking to him, except Kato Gonroku in Ep. 21. A hire can fail with "let us meet another time", probably when the workshop is full. Battle craftsmen are offered in that one battle only.

All of this comes from the scripts and has not yet been played through; if something doesn't fire, the episode or square is the first thing to doubt.
